7. **Button styles are inconsistent.** Filled, outlined, and border-only variants exist side by side. Standardize variants in `@hwp/core-ui/Button` (`primary`, `secondary`, `outline`, `ghost`) and audit usages.
8. **Accessibility is partial.** ARIA labels and semantic HTML appear sporadically. `@hwp/core-ui` must enforce WCAG 2.1 AA in every reusable block: heading hierarchy, alt text required (Payload schema-level), focus states, link semantics, keyboard navigation.
